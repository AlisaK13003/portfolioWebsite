Description

  • 2D RPG
  • Turn-Based Combat
  • Exploration
  • Character Bonds

Sealbound is a 2D pixel-art fantasy RPG centered around exploration, turn-based party combat, character relationships, and narrative progression. Players explore the village of Hearthwyn and surrounding dungeons, complete quests, build bonds with party members, and progress through a story centered around restoring ancient Seals that hold the world together.

Tools

  • Godot 4.6
  • GDScript
  • Git/GitHub
  • Jira

The game was developed in Godot using GDScript, with Git and GitHub for version control and Git LFS for managing larger game assets. Jira was used to organize development into sprints, assign tasks, track bugs, and keep milestones visible across the team.

Work Process

  • Sprint Planning
  • Scope Management
  • Team Coordination
  • Iterative Development

Sealbound was developed by a five-person team over roughly six months. We began with a detailed game design document defining the world, gameplay systems, characters, combat, progression, and overall scope.

From there, I translated the design into actionable features and milestones, organized development through Jira sprints, and coordinated implementation across programming, art, and level design. Features were developed iteratively through dedicated Git branches, integration, playtesting, debugging, and refinement.

The final prototype includes explorable environments, NPC schedules, branching dialogue, quests, shops, dungeon progression, turn-based combat, party management, and a character Bond system that connects relationships with gameplay progression.

My Role

  • Game Production
  • Game Design
  • Systems Design
  • Gameplay Programming

I served as the project's Game Producer, Designer, and Developer, overseeing both the creative direction and production of the game.

I created the original game concept and design documentation, defined systems such as combat, quests, character bonds, NPC behavior, progression, and dialogue, and managed the team's scope and development schedule. I also contributed directly to implementation in Godot, particularly gameplay systems, dialogue and narrative features, UI integration, debugging, and bringing separately developed systems together into a playable build.

As producer, I maintained our Jira backlog and sprint plans, ran team meetings, tracked milestones and blockers, coordinated responsibilities across the five-person team, and adjusted scope as development progressed to ensure we could deliver a complete playable prototype.
